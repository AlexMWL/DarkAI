1. Information Collection and Use
DarkAI is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data, conversation history, or generated images.
All Large Language Model (LLM) text processing is handled entirely by the local LLM manager on your device.

Image generation through the local diffusion wrapper is rendered completely offline.  
ZIP
The app utilizes a custom memory manager and personality matrix to remember context and adapt to you. This memory data is saved exclusively to your device's local storage and is never uploaded to any cloud server or third-party database.

2. Log Data
Because DarkAI operates completely offline, we do not collect typical internet log data (like IP addresses, browser types, or cookie data). The application features an internal log manager for crash reporting and debugging, but this data remains locally on your hardware unless you explicitly choose to export and share it with us.  
ZIP

3. Third-Party Services
DarkAI does not use any third-party services, APIs, or external tracking code that could collect information used to identify you. All processing, including document processing and prompt classification, happens right in the app.  
ZIP

4. Security
We value your trust in using our app. Since all conversation data, personality matrices, and images stay strictly on your device, the security of this data relies entirely on the built-in security and passcode protections of your Apple device.
