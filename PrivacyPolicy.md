# Privacy Policy for DarkAI

**Effective date:** August 10, 2026
**Applies to:** DarkAI 1.0 for iOS
**Developer:** Alexander Witlock ("we," "us," or "our")

## Summary

DarkAI has no account and no login. It does not sell personal data, and it contains no
analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no third-party crash reporting service, and no tracking of
any kind. Text and images are generated by models that run on your device, not on a server.

Two things, and only two, cause DarkAI to use the network, and you start both of them:

1. **Downloading a model file** that you picked from the in-app library, or that you imported.
2. **Internet Access** (Settings → Internet Access), a feature that is **off by default**. When
   it is on, the app still asks you before every individual search and only proceeds if you
   say yes.

Everything else — your conversations, your memories, your documents, and every image the app
generates — stays on your device.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or receive your personal information. There is no account, no user
profile, and no server that belongs to us. We have no ability to read your conversations,
your memories, your documents, or the images you generate.

The one category of data that ever leaves your device to a third party at our app's request is
the **text of a search you personally approved**, described in section 3. It is sent directly
from your device to the search provider, is not linked to your identity (there is no identifier
to link it to), and is never used for tracking or advertising. This is disclosed on our App
Store product page as *Search History → App Functionality, not linked to you, not used to
track you*.

## 2. What stays on your device

All of the following is written to DarkAI's private app container and never transmitted to us:

- Conversations, including your prompts and the model's responses.
- Long-term memories and the learned style profile the personality matrix builds from your
  messages.
- Documents and images you attach, and any text extracted from them.
- Images the app generates.
- Model files you download or import.
- Diagnostic logs and crash reports.
- Every app setting, including your Brave Search API key if you add one, which is stored in the
  iOS Keychain rather than in ordinary app storage.

This data is excluded from iCloud backup, so multi-gigabyte model files do not consume your
iCloud storage. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently.

## 3. What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you initiate it. Specifically:

**Model downloads.** When you choose a model from the built-in library, your device contacts
that model's host to fetch the file — Hugging Face for language models, and Civitai for some
diffusion models. This is a standard file download: no prompt, conversation, or personal data
is sent with it. The request is subject to that host's own privacy policy. No download ever
starts on its own.

**Internet Access — off by default.** You turn this on in Settings → Internet Access. Even
when it is on, DarkAI never searches on its own: before every individual search it asks you,
in the conversation, and sends nothing unless you say yes. If you say yes, the text of that one
search is sent — and nothing else. Not your conversation history, not your other messages, not
a device identifier, and not an account identifier, because the app has no account. Depending
on the question, that search text goes to:

| Provider | Used for | Privacy policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open-Meteo | Weather, and the city-name lookup behind it | https://open-meteo.com/en/terms |
| DuckDuckGo | General lookups | https://duckduckgo.com/privacy |
| Wikipedia (Wikimedia) | Encyclopedic lookups | https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy |
| Google News (RSS) | Current-events questions | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| Brave Search | General lookups, **only if you supply your own API key** | https://search.brave.com/help/privacy-policy |

If you add your own Brave Search API key, that traffic goes directly from your device to Brave
using your key, under Brave's privacy policy and your own agreement with them — the same
relationship as a model download with its host.

A search result that comes back is used only as reference material for the model that is still
running on your device. It is never a substitute for on-device generation, and your prompt is
never sent anywhere for processing.

Turning Internet Access off, or simply declining an individual search offer, means nothing
about that message ever leaves your device.

**Reports you choose to send.** If you file a content report or send a crash report, your own
mail app opens with the message pre-filled. Nothing is sent until you tap send, and you can
read, edit, or discard any part of it first. Reports sent this way arrive at
lostsoccerball@icloud.com and are used only to investigate the problem you reported.

**Anything you share or export.** If you use a share or export control, the content goes
wherever you send it.

## 4. Crash reports and diagnostic logs

DarkAI detects when it closes unexpectedly and writes a crash report to your device. A crash
report contains only technical information: the crash type and signal, a stack trace of
function names, the app version, your iOS version and device model, and how much memory was
free at the time. It does not contain your prompts, conversations, memories, documents, or
generated images.

The app also keeps a rolling local log of technical events — model loads, memory decisions,
download results, and whether the content filter blocked something (the category only, never
the text).

Neither is ever transmitted automatically. Attaching the diagnostic log to a report is off by
default and is always your choice, and you can read the full log before sending it. You can
view, clear, or disable crash reports and logs entirely in Settings → Safety & Legal and
Settings → Diagnostics.

## 5. Log data

We operate no server and therefore collect no server-side log data — no IP addresses, no
browser or device fingerprints, no cookies. The third parties listed in section 3 receive a
request directly from your device when you approve a search or start a download, and will
handle it under their own policies.

## 6. Third-party services

DarkAI embeds no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs. The
only third parties involved at all are the model hosts and search providers named in section 3,
each of which is contacted only as the direct result of an action you took.

Content-safety screening, optical character recognition, document processing, and prompt
classification all run locally on your device using Apple frameworks and on-device model
weights.

## 7. Security

Because conversations, memories, generated images, and model files stay on your device, their
security rests on your device's own protections — your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS
data protection. A Brave Search API key, if you provide one, is stored in the iOS Keychain.
Network requests the app does make use HTTPS.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

## 8. Children

DarkAI is rated 17+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information
from anyone, including children. Because we collect nothing and operate no account system, we
hold no data to delete on request — deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## 9. Your control

You can clear conversations, memories, learned personality data, generated images, downloaded
models, and diagnostic logs at any time from Settings. You can turn Internet Access off at any
time, and remove your Brave API key. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your
device, permanently.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always posted on this page
with its effective date, and the same policy text ships inside the app under Settings → Safety
& Legal. Material changes will be reflected in an app update. Continued use after a change
means you accept the updated policy.
